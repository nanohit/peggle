# Des6 visual and campaign review

Reviewed the main Alea sheet, Des1 and Des2 sheets, Des3 constructions, the final
Des5 sheet and representative Blast designs before implementation. The common
weakness of Des5 was a long run of receiving vessels and balances, despite seed
and route variation. Main Alea supplies stronger contrasts of timing, geometric
fields and ricochets. Blast supplies recognizable dotted figures as well as
coherent sweeping strokes. Earlier transfer systems remain useful episodes.

The first six new forms had the same dotted lattice behind their banks. That
was a visual repetition even when the curves differed. Replaced it with a woven
spine, elliptical target phrases, an outer rosette orbit, chevron field, lantern
constellations and a warped sail field. No source campaign was altered.

The initial greedy campaign beam starved rare families near its ending. Changed
the system to choose a portfolio first, then optimize its whole order. "Grown"
was also incorrectly treated as one Des3 formation; its numerical receiving
assemblies now define different mechanism families. A portal label no longer
hides a gravity scene for the purposes of spacing it from other cargo scenes.

Reviewed all final 48 initial pictures and native start/first/penultimate/final
frames. The sequence alternates broad banks, animated fields, concentrated
mechanical action and drawn figures. New rosettes visibly change access after
hits. Native cargo releases, retained loads, tipping and later clearing remain
real Game behavior, with no staged level rules. There is more whole-session
contrast than the Des5-only campaign; it is not achieved by stacking every
mechanic into each level.

Remaining limits: some static bank formations share a familiar visual language;
the campaign bank is finite; solver shot count is only a rough cadence input;
human aiming time and enjoyment are not measured. Old main levels retain their
intentional structural gray pieces and native special pegs; no new permanent
cargo balls or new peg classes were introduced. The client button builds new
itineraries from the proven bank, not new unchecked coordinates.
