# Des4 — review ledger

## Source review (before generation)

All 32 published Des3 images were inspected in four contact sheets. The dominant silhouette is a horizontal source shelf, two or three receiving bands and a vertical sprinkle. Curvature varies, but depth, target rhythm, side access, source location and transfer direction do not vary enough. Occupancy cosine and graph signatures credited details which did not change the player's first decision. A passed transfer intervention is not evidence of an interesting shot.

Inspected all 20 production Alea level geometries and 14 selected Blast levels: free bird, spinning star, gem tree, spider, jellyfish, propellers, fish, flowers, opera house, trees, roulette, spinning circles and two event variations. Sources were read without modification. The older small-radius Blast port images are source evidence, not the scale to publish.

Useful design observations:

- Alea rotates whole compound paths and supports hit-triggered spin/translation, not merely continuously floating decorations.
- Blast's contours organize the composition: fins, branches and concentric vessels define bounce choices and readable silhouettes.
- A large contour with an interior mechanism is a different spatial problem from a grid of mini-containers.
- Boundary banks can make aiming from a side useful. The two walls should not always be interchangeable empty escape lanes.
- Color runs and target clusters make different shot goals; alternating every few pegs on every assembly produces the same rhythm everywhere.
- A portal's orientation and exit velocity are part of the geometry. Placement must consider travel after the exit.
- Magnets influence movable cargo in this engine, not the white ball. Their utility has to be measured against cargo motion.

## Iteration 01 — shape laws and full-size native pegs

Thirty numerical previews, three seeds in each of ten domains, inspected directly. Created recursive branching, cubic fish profiles, harmonic lobed contours, articulated trees, oblique responsive panels, load-bearing frames, enclosed rotating cargo and unequal orbit packing. These are numerical constructors, not image models or stored coordinate templates.

What improved: silhouette is immediately different across domains; variable circle radii are visible; whole-path motion and native hit-trigger animations exist; horizontal, oblique and angular movement have a physical representation.

What is still inadequate:

- Fish tails cross the body tip; nested branches meet in crowded joints. Geometry must derive proper attachment junctions instead of excusing penetration.
- Tower slab/column contacts penetrate by 1.5 px; tall towers put another floor through some loose loads.
- Mobile landing brackets extend into the walls. Suspended forms are still too centered and too similar.
- Canopies need whole-tree scaling and more open branch tips; their lower half is mostly one bare trunk.
- A few domains are sparse and their orange assignment makes almost every peg a target. Target budgets should be coupled to actual structural content.
- Roulette, petals and some houses leave broad empty lower/upper areas. Optional related substructure should come from open contour sectors / releases, not a standard bottom catcher on every level.
- Exact placement is too fixed inside several domains. Vary dominant versus multiple forms and their embedding, not only small parameters.

Native first-angle and idle studies are kept separately. Geometry and machine findings guide the next revision; they do not certify this first batch.

## Iterations 02–03 — attachments and native clearing

Inspected all 33 previews in each revision and native initial / after-first / before-last / final frames for the studied laws. Iteration 02 studied eleven full native routes; iteration 03 studied twenty-two. Fish became a single closed cubic outline, branch junctions were trimmed and supported by round pegs, and tower floors/columns were computed as a support graph. All studied routes cleared, but this was insufficient for publication: a few roof junctions and ellipse blades still intersected, flowers needed extremely specific first angles, and the three-small-cups mobile read as another repeated container stack.

The next revision joined the roof into one true path, coupled blade reach to the vessel profile and replaced the symmetric mobile with one dominant or two unequal suspended shapes. Orbit placement became constrained disc packing rather than a fixed grid. Shoals vary from one large profile with gills to several uneven silhouettes.

## Iteration 04 — selection exposes weak interactions

36 previews, twelve laws. Pool: 72 candidates; 48 accepted, 24 rejected; 32 selected and replayed. Geometry improved, but hit animations on small tips often disappeared before exhibiting meaningful displacement. Canopy and some slalom candidates failed the observed-response gate. The initial mobile shapes still produced narrow first access. This catalog was retained as an intermediate artifact, not published as the final result.

## Iteration 05 — whole subtrees and numerical morphogenesis

39 previews, thirteen laws. Hit-triggered canopy motion now includes a whole major subtree with descendants, junctions and fruit, rather than an isolated terminal segment. Seven of the first eight canopy seeds showed the required motion. Morphogenesis combines two harmonic components, phase and aspect, derives surface attachment points, and packs child contours against circum-envelopes. It can generate an unfamiliar cellular silhouette without saved art coordinates.

Pool: 104 candidates; 75 accepted, 29 rejected; 32 selected. All 32 initial native frames were inspected. An independent full native search completed all 32, but a sampled rotation envelope found a flower crossing the right boundary after turning. The visual selection also revealed two remaining patterns: small houses confined to the bottom and portal routes ending in the same lower ring. Winning routes alone did not resolve these issues.

## Iteration 06 — tall supports and trajectory-derived outlets

Upper posts became latent physical bodies activated by actual hits, while bottom supports remain idle-stable. Taller stepped frames now survive waiting and collapse under the native collision system. The portal constructor chooses a landing region and solves for an estimated outlet velocity including gravity, then builds its outlet bank from that fan. Landing formations vary among an arc, fan and two ribbons.

Pool: 130 candidates; 87 accepted, 43 rejected. All ten portal candidates initially failed authored geometry: the bank started on the outlet tangent and the proposed ribbons crossed each other. A nominal 32-level selection with no portals was rejected as a final result. The failure led to geometry corrections, not removal of functional portal variety.

## Iteration 07 — final selection and direct review

The outlet bank starts forward along the outlet velocity; the two landing ribbons are parallel; landing placement avoids the input portal. Flower size is clamped against its complete rotational circum-envelope. Animation-envelope validation now precedes route acceptance. Portal candidates must show an actual native teleport, fields must change a fixed-policy cargo trace, hit-triggered groups must visibly move surviving members, and houses must show real support/floor displacement. Cached numerical routes can be reused only for identical generated geometry and unchanged native sources; current safety gates still run again.

Pool: 130 candidates; **93 accepted, 37 rejected**. Final selection: 32, two or three from every law. All 32 initial states and all 32 four-panel native winning routes were directly inspected in four initial contact sheets and eight route sheets. The catalog includes small and tall frames, one / two / three fish, recursive crowns with different headings, unequal packed orbit counts, open side-to-side channels, full-size rotating vessels, analytic grafts, suspended shapes and differently oriented portals. The native frames show side rebounds, release of loose internal loads, changed silhouettes after hit animations and genuine collapse. Portal jumps are not drawn as fictitious straight paths.

The independent search uses another collision RNG and idle time, and the native animator is sampled through 177 phases. All 32 independently clear and replay, with valid sampled envelopes. This is evidence of accessibility and native behavior, not a human enjoyment score or a continuous collision proof.

Remaining limitations from the review: ring-based forms still share an internal-load gesture; narrow closed shapes can require aiming into a partial opening; some trees expose gaps between the larger junction pegs and short branches; selected houses are mostly orthogonal; a four-frame strip cannot show all motion between shots. Future work should use session duration and tail-clearing frustration, vary support graphs beyond rectangular frames, and grow interactions across compatible laws. These are specific improvements to the generator, not reasons to fill every level with all mechanics.

## Autonomy boundary

The browser-compatible compiler takes a seed and builds new numerical formations synchronously without a model, network or lookup of this catalog. The laws and native mechanics are bounded by code. Default seeds choose a law; within it, parameters, recursive topology, placement, contour genes and attachments change. Arbitrary seeds are raw candidates, not automatically certified games. Published levels are generated, rejected/selected, reviewed and native-verified offline. Creating entirely new physics or learning design intent is not implemented.
