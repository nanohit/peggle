# Des5: orchestration, not another replacement vocabulary

## Failure of previous abstractions

Des2 has useful open shot spaces and compound paths but treats the leading mechanic as a late insertion into free space. Des3 has useful physical hinges, vessels and explicit transfers but embeds nearly every graph in the same 3–4 horizontal ranks. Des4 has useful silhouette synthesis and hit-triggered whole-object movement but makes a single constructor own nearly the entire board. These are three useful subsystems, not competing definitions of a level.

Changing object names or adding more constructors does not solve orchestration. Nor does filling every empty patch with another mechanism. Empty space can be a flight corridor, a motion envelope, an aiming opportunity or deliberate contrast. A secondary component needs a relationship to the existing system, not just a vacant rectangle.

## Working abstraction

1. A continuous design genome controls focus, physical response, direction change, branching, openness and structural density. It constrains the entire composition rather than choosing a saved scene.
2. A growing interaction graph gives subsystems input apertures, discharge regions, supported loads, rebound banks and response envelopes. Optional upstream/downstream/side growth follows those contracts. It does not impose a compulsory player sequence.
3. Geometry providers implement a requested role and envelope. Des2's open lanes, Des3's hinged receivers and Des4's analytic contours share the same contract. A large contour is one possible subsystem, never the universal level template.
4. Spatial search evaluates actual full-size geometry, reserved travel corridors, motion envelopes, target mass and the remaining complexity budget. Failed additions are omitted; the level is not shrunk to fit them. Omission is recorded as a design decision.
5. Material rhythm, curvature and target allocation are global, so subordinate forms echo the same intent. Providers retain their native physical behavior. Ratios measure actual structural contribution rather than count named toys.
6. Native Game traces distinguish a proposed connection from an observed transfer or rebound. A physically clearing yet disconnected composition is not automatically a successful orchestration. Native evidence, rendered states and direct visual review drive revisions.
7. The playlist balances continuous design traits and observed behavior across levels. Individual levels can be restrained or complex. They do not each owe the same list of mechanics.

## Preservation and scope

Older campaign data and generators remain intact. Des5 reuses numerical cubic baking, native hinges, collision geometry, native solver and parameterized shape construction. The grammar remains browser-compatible and deterministic. Existing orange clearing and normal ball size stay unchanged; no stages, new peg types or prescribed defeat rules are introduced. This file records intent; generated artifacts and native traces must establish whether the implementation actually achieves it.

## Iteration 01: evidence against an easy stopping point

24 deterministic proposals were rendered and directly reviewed. 18 passed the first native study; 6 were too small in target/material contribution. The accepted native routes had 1–4 observed graph links and coupled structural ink from 44% to 100%. This confirms that some transfers/rebounds occur, not that the spatial composition is good. Direct previews still showed empty source shelves, small repeated receiving cups and too little material across the board. This batch is not the final catalog.

Iteration 02 treats supply apertures as capability contracts, rejects supply nodes with no real load, restores enclosed moving contours alongside open contours, allows the same contour provider as a subordinate node, and derives portal outlets from a receiving aperture. Optional field/bumper couplers implement a specific connection. Structural target mass and color runs are now allocated globally, so tiny satellites do not each become a compulsory cleanup objective. A real nested bearing can be proposed inside a compatible enclosure; containment is distinct from a forbidden surface overlap.


## Iterations 02–04: measured corrections

Iteration 02: 24 stored proposals, 20 accepted, 2 insufficient compositions, 1 unused field and 1 unused portal. All four-state native sheets were reviewed. Curved receivers still dominated the same repeated cascade. The declared branching gene was found to be inert and was corrected rather than described as functional.

Iteration 03: the discharge frontier now partitions broad fans, weights continuation versus branching and can rejoin compatible receivers. Boundary fields emit harmonic coefficient programs, asymmetric aperture profiles and multi-turn spline skeletons. Support networks return real loads and ports; a pocket is an affordance for a bearing, not an instruction to fill every opening. Of 24 raw proposals, 14 were accepted, 5 lost targets while idle, 3 lacked sufficient composition, 1 had an unused portal and 1 had no observed orchestration. All initial sheets and route sheets were reviewed. Unsupported post gaps in the new support network were corrected by joining floor spans and bounding stagger; no individual level coordinates were edited.

The final source revision generated 96 candidates autonomously: 70 native passes, 26 rejections (10 insufficient composition, 6 unused fields, 5 unused portals, 2 idle target losses, 2 unobserved orchestration, 1 route-rhythm failure). A visually inspected pass had an isolated dominant spiral while secondary nodes interacted; playlist eligibility now requires an observed relationship involving the focal node and at least 50% coupled node ink. The selected 32 have 15 degree topologies, average 34.53 pegs, 42.85% focal node ink and 91.36% observed coupled node ink. All 32 first states and four-state routes were directly reviewed. All 32 independently cleared and replayed with a different RNG and start time. These are accessibility and interaction measurements, not a declaration that every composition is equally fun.

Paired low/high branch intent on 12 unpublished seeds produced 21 distinct outputs in 24 runs; 9 of 12 pairs changed structure, and mean branches rose from 0.33 to 0.75. A physically constrained frontier may refuse growth; unchanged pairs are recorded rather than hidden. The browser module and worker run the same deterministic synthesis with bounded intent and initial geometry checks. Full physical catalog selection is autonomous offline. Public `/des5` serves the ready catalog in the existing production art. Legacy campaigns were checked against their original commit and are byte-identical; the separate research checkout was not edited.

Remaining growth points: several families still echo U-shaped receivers and spline banks; analytic discharge fans are approximations of changing native bodies; dormant native body contacts and authored-aperture crossings are weaker evidence than a causal ablation. Expanding the physical operation vocabulary or learning better placement priors should preserve the common intent, port contracts and space/response budgets. It should not introduce a new unrelated campaign or fill space merely to pass a component checklist.
