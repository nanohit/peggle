# Des3 / destruction_systems 0.1

Default generation grows a bounded layered transfer graph, not a selection of complete levels. `planComposition({seed})` expands continuation, branching, merging, crossed transport and side growth. The numerical compiler derives source, receiving and discharge ports, free corridors, vessel profiles, hinge geometry, cargo and native transport. Six named scenes remain explicit calibration cases; the default is `grown`.

Forms are synthesized within an extensible vocabulary. A vessel's cubics share the bottom tangent, with variable mouth, depth, asymmetric bottom and discharge side. Opposed spans derive leaf width and opening from the node envelope. Branching/radial shapes use numerical paths. Native size remains radius 8.5, brick 34×10.2 except intentional mechanical spans. Existing ordinary orange clearing, timed clearing, native destruction, magnets, portals, bumpers and animations are unchanged. No new peg types, stages, sequence objectives or defeat rules.

`grammar.js` is deterministic and browser-compatible. Build generation/selection requires no model or human placement. Its limited grammar cannot invent new native physics. To extend it, add compatible surface constructions or graph rewrite rules, and retain their interface/clearance contracts. A seed alone does not certify a new level.

`evaluate.mjs` rejects authored overlap, missing physical transfers, out-of-board bodies, autonomous target loss, sampled moving overlap and weak native first shots. Full real Game search plus independent replay precede acceptance. Source-release interventions check cargo handoff. Magnetic cargo must change by ≥10px without its field or differ in exit from the board; portal transport must demonstrate real cargo or white-ball relocation. Intervention and launcher-ball runs are distinct evidence.

`build-catalog.mjs` uses isolated Node workers to evaluate a deterministic pool and checkpoints source fingerprints. Winning-route reuse additionally requires an identical geometry key and native runtime fingerprint; probes are recomputed after evaluation-policy changes. Selection covers mechanics and minimizes occupancy/structural repetition. `robustness.mjs` runs adaptive search with another collision RNG/start time, and records fixed-angle helper ablation. `experiments.mjs` instruments native contacts and actual portal transfers, never synthetic animations.

Publication is an offline catalog at `/des3`, all unlocked, through the existing production Alea renderer. There is no on-demand client generation in this route. Future browser workers can run the geometry compiler cheaply; full native search is the expensive part. `/des2` retains its existing client worker and catalog. The photographed four-post bridge was identified in `/des`; all occurrences and its source grammar have the posts removed.

Numerical contact sheets and native trajectory frames are inspected without computer use. Twenty-five motion phases cover one full animation cycle but are not a continuous collision proof. Full clearing and handoff evidence do not measure human enjoyment, retention or phone input. Source campaigns/research are retained, apart from the specifically requested guide-post fix.
